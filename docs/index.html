About the Logo

Anatomy of the Modelized Glyph

M / 01

The Formless

Raw, untamed ideas remain unformed. The jagged, expanding path reflects the primal chaos of a thought at its first strike. Such energy is potent in its purest form, awaiting definition.

O / 02

The Aperture

A lens isolates the precise fragment from a mass of scattered thoughts. It establishes focus and secures the correct piece within the void. This serves as the initial threshold an idea must pass to become tangible.

D / 03

The Structure

The structure assigned to an idea once it moves through the opening is never fixed. It offers clear direction to abstract concepts and supplies the momentum needed to realize them.

One Whole

The Modelized Glyph

Three separate parts form one whole. The logo acts as a visual map of the creative journey: locating focus in disorder and turning it into something tangible. It offers visible evidence of one principle: The most powerful ideas begin formless — I give them structure.
